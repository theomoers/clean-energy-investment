ADJUSTED SCORES AND TARGET
Use the previously specified logistic state + nine-covariate propensity e,
estimated from X and party only. Reproduce its original values.
Gamma = m1-m0 + T/e*(DeltaY-m1) - (1-T)/(1-e)*(DeltaY-m0).
Compute h*Gamma in bounded form, h=e(1-e), avoiding numerical inverse-weight
explosion. This is an AIPW-type conditional difference-in-changes score.
It is not automatically an ATT against an untreated group: Democrats also lose
majority alignment. Causal interpretation still requires conditional parallel
trends and no differential contemporaneous shocks.
Because the overlap target depends on the estimated propensity, do NOT claim
the whole overlap estimand is doubly robust/orthogonal without qualification.
Refit e in inference to incorporate target/weight estimation. The trained
outcome models and ranking are held fixed for honest evaluation inference.

CONTINUOUS VALIDATION
Primary ranking metric: exact weighted QINI integral int_0^1 q*TOC(q)dq, using
fractional inclusion at weighted rank boundaries and averaged ties. Equivalent
to overlap-weighted covariance of Gamma with ascending weighted midrank.
Positive means the frozen ranking puts larger conditional-change effects higher.
Use two-sided state-bootstrap t(9) inference, Holm across three outcomes.
Also report the overlap-weighted adjusted average, and the original frozen
high-minus-low contrast using Gamma scores rather than raw changes.
Secondary summary: overlap-weighted projection of Gamma onto standardized log
geographic area and log prior clean capacity, with evaluation-state fixed
effects. Test both modifier slopes jointly; Holm across three joint tests and
separately across six individual slopes. Coefficients are effect associations,
not causal effects of the modifiers.
Use 999 evaluation-state bootstrap draws, training frequencies held at one,
evaluation states resampled with multiplicity. Refit full-design propensity
each draw, recompute tilts, scores, weighted ranks, means and projections.
Require >=95% numerical success for intervals. Report placebo/trend/IPW
sensitivities together, without selecting a favorable variant.

GROUP-AWARE DESIGN AUDIT (NO OUTCOME-BASED SELECTION)

The latter changes the group definition/estimand, not the learned ranking.
No hard area exclusions and no demand for identical states across both groups.

1. Pooled penalized propensity models on all 101 evaluation areas:

2. Convex calibration audit:
   Normalize each party/group cell to mass one. Minimize sum of squared weights,
   subject to all nine covariates AND state-indicator within-group SMDs <=
   {.10,.20,.30}; primary target .10. Per-area positive lower bound .01/n_cell;
   upper bound min(3/n_cell,.20), enforcing Kish ESS >=max(5,n_cell/3).
   Solve linear feasibility first, then the quadratic objective. Report
   infeasibility rather than relaxing constraints after seeing outcomes.
   This defines a calibrated common-support group target, not the original ATO.

For every candidate: report nonzero areas, group/party ESS, max normalized

Only passing candidates receive subsequent effect inference; all others remain
