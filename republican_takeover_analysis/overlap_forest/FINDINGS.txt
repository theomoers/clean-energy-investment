OVERLAP-TARGETED CAUSAL FOREST: EMPIRICAL HANDOFF
2026-11-06

BOTTOM LINE

The area-level forest retains all 196 eligible areas in 25 states, rather than
66 matched areas in 19 states. It does NOT establish more precise average
effects or validated causal heterogeneity. Overall weighting balances measured
means very closely, but that balance does not survive inside the learned
high/low groups. Attempts to restore within-group comparison support fail.

This result is not evidence of zero effects or homogeneous effects. The
overlap-targeted forest is a useful exploratory extension, not grounds for
claiming that the confounding problem is solved.

SAMPLE AND IMPLEMENTATION

- Same frozen data, political assignment, fixed geography and years as before.
- 110 Republican and 86 Democratic areas, including zero-project areas.
- Post-period counts: 922 clean-power and 113 manufacturing announcements
  across all eligible areas (741/181 clean and 78/35 manufacturing in R/D areas).
  These are project counts, not independent political experiments.
- Weighting's Kish effective sample sizes are 50.71 Republican and 33.98
  Democratic areas. The weighted effective number of states is about 16.47.
- Nine pre-2022 adjustment variables plus state indicators in the propensity.
- Area-level GRF causal forest, not duplicated pair rows. Four predetermined
  effect modifiers; all nine covariates and state indicators enter the auxiliary
  outcome model. Honest trees, 4000 trees, state clusters, fixed seeds/settings.
- Propensities are supplied to the residualized forest; no second set of
  arm-dependent overlap weights is passed as training sample weights.
- Native GRF overlap aggregation is an overlap-targeted residual regression;
  it is not simply an average of the forest's individual effect predictions.
- Full-sample propensities use X and party, not outcomes. They are not fully
  cluster-cross-fitted nuisance estimates. The auxiliary outcome model uses
  cluster-OOB predictions, which do not learn an unseen state's fixed intercept.
  This is model-assisted estimation, not a claimed fully cluster-cross-fitted,
  doubly robust DiD procedure.

AVERAGE RESULTS: EXACT LOGISTIC OVERLAP WEIGHTING

Outcome per area-year          Estimate    95% interval           p
Clean-power announcements      -0.428      [-1.304,  0.448]       .324
Manufacturing announcements    -0.034      [-0.331,  0.262]       .813
Net wind/solar capacity MW      19.12       [-14.14, 52.38]        .247

These reproduce the earlier overlap-weighted effects AND uncertainty. The
state bootstrap refits weights and preserves repeated-state multiplicity.
All three Holm-adjusted political-effect tests are nonsignificant.

The model-assisted native GRF overlap scores are also nonsignificant:
clean -0.479 [-1.428, 0.470]; manufacturing -0.039 [-0.303, 0.226];
capacity 20.70 MW [-11.82, 53.22]. These intervals condition on the nuisance
fits and are supplementary, not substituted for the refitted-weight bootstrap.

The exact-weighting standard errors are larger than in matching:
clean 0.424 versus 0.353; manufacturing 0.144 versus 0.084; capacity 16.12
versus 11.27 MW. Different target populations prevent treating this as a pure
efficiency experiment, but there is no observed precision improvement.

OVERALL CONFOUNDING DIAGNOSTICS

Maximum absolute SMD on the nine included covariate means: 4.1e-9.
This is mean balance, not equality of complete distributions or unobservables.

Refitted-weight joint pre-period tests:
clean-power p=.303; manufacturing p=.139; capacity p=.273.
These do not reject differential pre-trends. They do not establish equivalent
trends; power is limited and early pre-outcomes already enter the design.
The 2022-minus-2021 placebo, trend-adjusted changes and exclusion-of-2022
results are saved alongside the main results.

HELD-OUT FOREST VALIDATION

The fixed split uses 95 training areas in 15 states and 101 evaluation areas in
10 other states. Training outcomes and evaluation outcomes are state-disjoint.
Training OOB predictions weighted by e(1-e) define the median cutoff. The
evaluation cutoff is NOT redefined to force equally sized groups.

Outcome          High/low areas   High-minus-low    Diagnostic 95% interval
Clean power          17 / 84          0.454         [-3.096,   4.005]
Manufacturing        76 / 25         -0.087         [-0.412,   0.238]
Capacity             62 / 39        -32.33 MW        [-122.24, 57.58]

All three high-minus-low tests are nonsignificant; Holm p-values are all 1.
These are raw overlap-weighted held-out comparisons, not doubly robust
subgroup scores. Their causal interpretation additionally requires within-group
comparability. The following failures mean the displayed intervals are
diagnostics, not validated evidence about causal heterogeneity:

1. CLEAN POWER:
   High group effective sizes are only 4.23 R and 3.67 D areas.
   Max within-group covariate SMD=1.18; max state-indicator SMD=3.21.

2. MANUFACTURING:
   Low group effective Democratic size is 4.88 areas and has zero observed
   post-period Democratic manufacturing announcements.
   Max within-group covariate SMD=1.13; max state-indicator SMD=1.13.

3. CAPACITY:
   Both groups pass the prespecified minimum area/ESS support check.
   Max within-group covariate SMD=.46; max state-indicator SMD=.63.
   Thus balance still fails the .10-SD credibility threshold.

No outcome passes BOTH the prespecified support and balance requirements.
Mean balance overall does not imply balance within a forest-defined subgroup.
Failure of these raw subgroup comparisons is not a proof that conditional
heterogeneity cannot exist or that every individual forest prediction is zero.

WITHIN-GROUP RECALIBRATION ATTEMPT

Requiring the same evaluation states to contain both parties in both learned
groups leaves only one common state for clean power, two for manufacturing,
and four for capacity. Re-estimating nine-covariate/state overlap models within
these smaller groups leads to separation/convergence problems or ESS near
one. No recalibrated comparison meets the support requirements, so no repaired
heterogeneity interval is claimed.

WHAT THIS SUPPORTS

Use overlap weighting as a broader-sample average-effect comparison and retain
matching as a robustness design. The current forest may remain an exploratory
ML component, but weighting has not earned a stronger heterogeneity claim.
Do not replace the paper's cautions with a claim that larger nominal N solved
confounding or found no effects.

The estimand is still the Republican-minus-Democratic response to the national
majority transition, among overlap areas, conditional on weighted parallel
trends and no differential contemporaneous shocks. Both groups change majority
status. It is not the effect of the IRA/45X credit, not gain versus an unaffected
group, and not causal effects of individual covariates. Geography-consistent
resource, grid, socioeconomic and incentive-exposure variables remain missing.
