Overlap-targeted 2023 change forest: exploratory extension, 2026-11-06

Earlier matching and overlap-average results have been inspected. This is not
preregistered. Freeze this specification before estimating new forest rankings.
Do not edit manuscripts, legacy outputs or extension/results_current.

Population: all 196 eligible fixed areas in 25 mixed-party states, including
zero-project areas. Same dated political assignment and 2019-22/2023-24 changes.
Outcomes: clean-power announcements, manufacturing announcements, net wind/solar
MW. Also report 2022-minus-2021 placebo, trend adjustment and exclusion of 2022.
No new covariates invented for geographic cores.

Propensity: same unpenalized logistic state indicators + nine pre-2022 design
covariates. Full-sample weights must reproduce prior weights and effects.
Full and training-sample propensity models use X and party, never outcomes.
All three outcomes use the same sample/design, no significance-based selection.

Forest: area-level GRF causal forest, not duplicated matched-pair rows.
Four modifiers: log prior clean capacity, fossil capacity, clean announcements,
area. Adjustment set: all nine design covariates plus state. Auxiliary outcome
regression forest has 4000 trees and state clusters; use its out-of-bag
predictions (own-state observations excluded). Outcome nuisance predictions for
an unseen state cannot estimate its fixed intercept; do not claim otherwise.
State adjustment enters the propensity and weighted evaluation; correct model
and conditional parallel trends are still assumptions. Propensity is estimated
in sample using baseline X/party to preserve state/mean balance; this is not a
fully cluster-cross-fitted doubly robust DiD estimator.

causal_forest: 4000 trees, honesty TRUE, sample.fraction .5, min.node.size 5,
seed 72311, two threads, state clusters. W.hat is fitted propensity, NOT .5.
Do not additionally pass arm-dependent overlap weights as sample.weights.
Residualized splitting naturally emphasizes treatment variation. Report native
GRF overlap average as a model-assisted diagnostic and t(G-1) intervals; native
SE conditions on nuisance fits. The primary average comparison remains exact
logistic overlap weighting with state bootstrap refitting weights.

Meaning: conditional Republican-minus-Democratic response in investment changes
among overlap areas, under conditional weighted parallel trends and no
differential contemporaneous shocks. Both parties change majority status.
This is NOT a national IRA effect or separately identified gain-vs-unaffected
effect. CATE modifiers are not identified causal investment drivers.

Validation: fixed numpy RNG seed 48123 permutes sorted 25 states; first floor(.4G)
are held out. Train outcome models and ranking forest on training states ONLY;
refit propensity on training baseline/party data. No held-out outcomes enter
ranking or cutoff. Cutoff is training OOB prediction median weighted by e(1-e).
Do not force a new evaluation cutoff if one group is sparse.

Primary evaluation: high-minus-low exact-overlap weighted relative change using
the previously verified full-design weights (no held-out outcomes in weights).
Evaluate high/low effects, earlier placebo and trend sensitivity. Bootstrap
held-out states 999 times conditional on fixed training data/rankings, refitting
full-design propensity with training-state frequency one and resampled
evaluation-state multiplicities. Require >=4 areas/arm, >=5 Kish ESS/arm,
>=3 contributing states/group and >=4 evaluation states overall for a supported
group comparison. Report raw point estimates even if support fails, clearly
marked not inferentially reliable. Holm across three outcome ranking tests.

Confounding audit: within each learned group report all nine weighted SMDs,
state-share imbalance, ESS, event counts, contributing states and pre-placebo.
Primary credibility threshold: max absolute SMD <=.10 including state indicators;
nonrejection of a placebo does not establish equivalent trends.

Secondary design-only repair: restrict to evaluation states having both parties
in both high/low groups, then separately refit logistic overlap weights on all
nine covariates plus state within each group. Report convergence/separation,
ESS and losses. If >=95% of 999 bootstrap draws do not converge/support both
groups, withhold interval. Do not select the version with favorable p-values.

Weighted pre-trends: annual contrasts relative to 2022, full eligible sample,
with refitted-weight state bootstrap covariance and joint F tests over pre-years.
Capacity uses available 2017-22 history. Report all non-rejections as low-power
diagnostics; early pre-outcomes already enter design.

Reproduce into fresh output directory; hash inputs/code, compare deterministic
CSV outputs, independently check full-weight contrasts and score algebra.
Figures: weighted annual diagnostics and held-out ranking comparisons.
Deliver empirical findings and code; do not revise the paper automatically.
