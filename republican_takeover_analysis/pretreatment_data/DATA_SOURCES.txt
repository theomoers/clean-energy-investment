ADDITIONAL PRE-TREATMENT DATA

All 98 downloaded source responses (approximately 383 MB) are preserved under
raw/. Exact request URLs, reference periods, retrieval times, sizes and SHA256
hashes are in download_manifest.json. No account or API key is required to
replicate the frozen downloads. The live Census API returned a missing-key
page, so official Census bulk files were used instead of an unofficial mirror.

SOCIOECONOMIC CONDITIONS
Source: Census ACS 2017-2021 five-year estimates, released December 8, 2022.
https://www.census.gov/newsroom/press-kits/2022/acs-5-year.html
https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/summary_file/2021/table-based-SF/data/5YRData/
Tables: B01003 (population), B17001 (poverty), B15003 (education), B23025
(civilian labor force/unemployment), B19025 (aggregate household income) and
B11001 (households). Five controls: log population density, poverty rate,
unemployment rate, college share among adults 25+, and log mean household
income in 2021 dollars. B15003 cells 22-25 define bachelor's degree or higher.

Allocate additive tract counts by area fraction inside each fixed geographic
core, then form ratios. These are approximate spatially interpolated estimates,
not official estimates for these custom cores. Tract-area coverage is at least
99.91% in every core. Counts other than aggregate income cover the entire
observed population. For aggregate income, use only households in tracts with
reported income totals. Population coverage for those totals is at least 94.24%
(CA-22); coverage is recorded per variable in acs_core_audit.csv. Do not treat
missing Census sentinels as zero or average tract medians. ACS sampling error
and spatial-interpolation error are not propagated into effect intervals.

RENEWABLE RESOURCES
NASA POWER monthly regional API, 2010-2021 (88 raw grid responses).
https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/services/api/temporal/monthly/
ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DWN: surface solar radiation, kWh/m2/day, 1-degree cells.
WS50M: 50-meter wind speed, m/s, 0.625-degree longitude x 0.5-degree latitude.
Weight daily-average monthly values by calendar days and intersect native cells
with fixed cores in equal-area coordinates. Duplicate tile-edge cells are
deduplicated and their values checked. Every core has complete grid coverage.
These are coarse physical-resource proxies from retrospective NASA products.
They are not engineering-grade site assessments, turbine-height wind output,
or data files frozen at their historical publication dates. Only observations
ending in 2021 enter the measures.

GRID PROXIMITY (NOT SPARE INTERCONNECTION CAPACITY)
Official EIA-860 2021 annual archive:
https://www.eia.gov/electricity/data/eia860/
https://www.eia.gov/electricity/data/eia860/archive/xls/eia8602021.zip
Use plants with 2021 operable generators (OP, SB, OS, OA), operating year <=2021,
valid contiguous-US coordinates, and at least one reported grid voltage >=115kV.
There are 11,284 operable plants in the source; 454 lack a usable reported
voltage, and 3,545 qualifying high-voltage connection points enter the proxy.
Grid_node_audit.csv records inclusion and missingness for every source plant.

Compute area-weighted distance to the nearest point and fraction of core area
within 25km, using 133,471 polygon-clipped five-km quadrature cells in EPSG5070.
New controls are log(1+mean distance in km) and area fraction within 25km.
Connection points outside a core can serve it. Distances are not restricted
to points inside a district or state. The grid file is a historical annual
archive downloaded today; later archival revisions cannot be ruled out.
This does NOT measure all transmission lines/substations, hosting capacity,
available queue capacity, congestion, or an interconnection offer. A verified
historical nationwide measure of those quantities remains missing.

IRA EXPOSURE PROXIES, NOT REALIZED SUBSIDIES
Treasury historical lists:
https://home.treasury.gov/policy-issues/tax-policy/data-transparency/all-treasury-generated-energy-communities-data-sets
https://home.treasury.gov/system/files/131/EC_CC_V1.xlsx
https://home.treasury.gov/system/files/131/EC_MSA_FFE_V1.xlsx

Coal exposure: share of core area in tracts containing a documented mine
closure (since 2000) or coal-unit retirement (since 2010), dated before 2022,
or directly adjoining such tracts. Restrict directly affected tracts to the
first Treasury list and separately dated closures in the existing MSHA/EIA
audit. Adjacency includes corner contacts as specified by IRS, using the
existing 2020 tract map. There are 3,945 exposure tracts nationally. Closure
dates and locations are retrospectively reconstructed. No post-2021 closure
enters. This is not a full official historical eligibility determination.

Fossil-employment exposure: core-area share in statistical areas appearing
in the first FFE list, based on employment through 2020. This captures only
the fossil-employment limb, NOT the annual unemployment requirement. Lists
were published in 2023; the underlying employment facts precede the takeover
and IRA. We use them as reconstructed predetermined conditions, not as
designations investors could already observe in 2021.

Industrial exposure: log(1+2020 establishments per 1,000km2) in NAICS 335911,
335912 (batteries) and 334413 (semiconductors, including solar cells).
https://www.census.gov/data/datasets/2020/econ/cbp/2020-cbp.html
https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/cbp/datasets/2020/cbp20co.zip
Use establishments rather than suppressed employment. County establishment
densities are interpolated into cores by area. These broad industries are
related to some 45X products but are NOT an exact eligibility crosswalk.
Semiconductors include non-solar products, and many other eligible components
are not captured. This is an industrial-footprint confounding control only.

Rules applicable to study period, not subsequent legislative amendments:
https://www.irs.gov/irb/2023-29_IRB
https://www.irs.gov/irb/2024-51_IRB
Energy-community bonuses concern power credits; Section 45X is a distinct
manufacturing production credit. We do not assign the former bonus to 45X.
Brownfields, realized credits, 48C awards, post-2023 unemployment and new
post-takeover manufacturing facilities are not used as controls.
