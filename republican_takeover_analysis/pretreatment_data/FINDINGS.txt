ADDITIONAL PRE-TREATMENT DATA AND CAUSAL FOREST AUDIT
2026-11-07

BOTTOM LINE

Downloaded the additional public data and added twelve predetermined measures
to the original nine controls. This improves measured overall confounder
balance considerably. It does not deliver a credible, validated finding of
heterogeneous political effects or evidence that the effect is zero.

The richer data reveal that the original weighting left major socioeconomic
differences: under the original unpenalized nine-control weights, poverty and
unemployment differed by about 1.04 and .98 standard deviations, respectively.
This is useful evidence about previously unmeasured confounding, not a reason
to omit the new controls when they make inference harder.

DATA ADDED

98 official source responses, approximately 383 MB, with URLs and SHA256 hashes.
- ACS 2017-2021: population density, poverty, unemployment, college attainment
  and mean household income, constructed for the actual fixed geographic cores.
- NASA POWER 2010-2021 solar radiation and 50m wind-speed grids.
- EIA-860 2021 high-voltage plant-connection proximity (two controls).
- Pre-2022 coal-closure exposure, pre-2021 fossil-employment exposure, and
  2020 battery/semiconductor industrial density (three IRA-related proxies).

All 196 areas remain. Resource-grid coverage is complete; ACS tract-area
coverage is at least 99.91%. Read DATA_SOURCES.txt for definitions and caveats.
Grid proximity is NOT available interconnection capacity. The IRA variables
are reconstructed pre-existing conditions, NOT credit claims or exact 45X
eligibility. Later published Treasury lists are not described as information
available to firms in 2021. No post-treatment awards/projects enter controls.

WHAT WAS ESTIMATED

Retained the 2023 Republican takeover design, outcome definitions and fixed
state-disjoint split (95 training areas/15 states; 101 evaluation areas/10
states). Republicans gain House-majority alignment; Democrats lose it. Neither
group is unaffected by the national turnover. This is not an estimate of the
IRA's overall effect or of causal effects of resources, income or grid access.

Compared the nine-control baseline with a prespecified 21-control model.
The expanded forest allows all 21 controls to modify effects. Forests and
arm-specific outcome models use training outcomes only. The old forest ranking
is also evaluated with expanded adjustment, separating adjustment from
relearning. Ridge outcome tuning is leave-one-training-state-out only.
Propensity estimation uses party/X in all areas, not a fully state-cross-fitted
design. Because the overlap tilt depends on the estimated propensity, the
entire target is not automatically doubly robust. Bootstrap intervals hold
trained forests and outcome models fixed and do not capture every source of
training or covariate-measurement uncertainty.

The evaluation states have been examined in earlier analyses. They are held
out of forest fitting, but are NOT a newly untouched confirmatory sample.
Results from this methodological extension should be treated as exploratory.

OVERLAP AND BALANCE

The unpenalized expanded model nearly separates the full sample and completely
separates the 95 training observations (fitted propensities essentially zero
or one). Full-sample effective counts fall to roughly 12 R and 10 D. This may
partly reflect high-dimensional finite-sample overfitting; it is not proof
that the population has no common support.

The prespecified outcome-free rule therefore uses a fixed .01 ridge penalty
on standardized covariates, with unpenalized state intercepts, in both the
baseline and expanded comparison. This sacrifices exact covariate mean balance
for stability and changes the estimated overlap target. Results must not be
presented as the same estimand as the old unpenalized overlap analysis.

Evaluation effective states                  7.62                6.35

Balance improves, but the .10-SD standard is not reached even overall. Retaining
all rows is not the same as retaining their statistical information. The
expanded forest's rank-half subgroup audit has minimum party/group ESS of
4.80 (clean power), 4.57 (manufacturing), and 3.79 (capacity). Residual covariate
and state differences remain. No candidate meets all prespecified subgroup
balance, effective-sample and concentration requirements. Do not promote
subgroup or state-level causal effect claims from these fits.

FOREST HETEROGENEITY VALIDATION

QINI is an effect-ranking statistic, NOT an average policy effect. Positive
values indicate better correspondence between predicted and adjusted effects.
All three outcomes and both rankings are reported; Holm correction covers
the six prespecified primary ranking tests.

Relearned expanded forest, expanded adjustment:
Outcome               QINI         Pointwise 95% CI          Raw p    Holm p
Clean power            .087       [-.454, .628]               .724     1.000
Manufacturing          .054       [ .007, .101]               .030      .177
Net capacity          1.263       [-8.642,11.168]              .780     1.000

The manufacturing ranking is an exploratory signal worth documenting, but it
does not survive the prespecified correction and its subgroups lack the
required balance/support. It is not a confirmed causal heterogeneity result.

Holding the old ranking fixed while adding adjustment gives raw QINI p-values
.814, .322 and .294, respectively. No old-ranking result is validated either.
Placebo and trend-adjusted results are saved, not used to select a specification.

None rejects the joint zero pretrend restrictions. These tests have limited
power, and 2022 itself overlaps the beginning of the IRA. Nonrejection is not
proof of parallel counterfactual trends or absence of concurrent IRA shocks.
The expanded tests do not provide stronger affirmative identification evidence.

INTERPRETATION FOR THE PAPER

We can defend a richer, transparent econometrics-plus-ML analysis of whether
political effects vary, and report that this sample does not robustly validate
such heterogeneity. We cannot defend confirmed political subgroup effects,
"no effect," or claims that split-importance rankings identify causal drivers
of investment. Covariates can predict investment and effect variation without
their own effects being causally identified.

The remaining bottleneck is comparable Republican/Democratic areas and
independent political variation, not merely adding more control columns.
Stronger evidence would require additional defensible turnover cohorts or
another credible source of political variation, more independent states/events,
and better historical grid-capacity/eligibility measures. Any simpler covariate
representation or revised forest should be specified before further evaluation,
not selected because it yields a smaller p-value on these same states.

REPLICATION AND FILES

From repository root, using clean-investment-causal:
conda run -n clean-investment-causal python republican_takeover_analysis/pretreatment_data/replicate.py --out /private/tmp/takeover-new-data-rebuild

The output directory must not already exist. Frozen raw downloads are reused;
--download fetches any missing sources. Generated covariate audit files in this
separate analysis directory are rebuilt; legacy files and manuscripts are not
edited. The R package library defaults to /private/tmp/clean-grf-r46 and can be
set with TAKEOVER_R_LIB. Required R package: grf; Python environment includes
geopandas, pandas, numpy, scipy, statsmodels, matplotlib, requests and openpyxl
(the latter is used only to read official source spreadsheets).

comparison.csv: all primary, placebo and trend-adjusted estimates.
support_comparison.csv, subgroup_comparison.csv: effective samples and balance.
prediction_metrics.csv: evaluation errors on common observations.
pretrend_estimates.csv, pretrend_joint_tests.csv: full-panel diagnostics.
expanded/{old,expanded}/: every subgroup weighting candidate, not only examples.
raw/ and download_manifest.json: frozen downloaded data and exact source URLs.
covariates.csv and *_audit.csv: constructed controls and coverage checks.
results_current/ and replication/: independently rerun results.
verification.json: source hashes, independent score/interval checks and rerun
comparison. A fresh output-directory rerun reproduces all 44 result CSV tables
within 1e-9; 230 source, numerical and replication checks pass. No manuscript,
figure or legacy result was changed.
