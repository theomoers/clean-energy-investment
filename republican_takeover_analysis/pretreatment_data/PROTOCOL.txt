Additional pre-treatment controls, frozen 2026-11-07 before expanded-model results

Retain the existing 196 fixed district cores, treatment, outcomes and 95/101
training/evaluation state split. Do not rewrite any manuscript or legacy result.
New measurements end in 2021 wherever possible, before both the IRA and takeover.

Sources and features, chosen without new outcome estimates:
1. ACS 2017-2021 tract counts: population density, poverty, unemployment,
   college attainment and mean household income. Allocate additive counts by
   tract/core overlap area, then form rates. This is areal interpolation, not
   an exact census of residents inside a core. Never average tract medians.
2. NASA POWER 2010-2021 regional solar radiation and 50m wind speed, averaged
   over months and area-weighted over native cells intersecting each core.
   Retrospective physical-resource measures, not historical data-release files.
3. EIA-860 2021 operating-plant interconnection voltages: area-weighted distance
   to a >=115kV plant connection and fraction within 25km. These measure
   proximity to reported plant connection points, NOT spare grid capacity,
   queues, or all substations/transmission lines. Missing voltages are audited.
4. IRA exposure proxies: share of area in coal-closure tracts or adjoining
   tracts with documented pre-2022 closures; share in fossil-employment
   statistical areas based on pre-2021 employment; density of 2020 battery/
   semiconductor establishments. These are predetermined physical/industrial
   exposure proxies, not realized subsidies or exact 45X eligibility. Later
   Treasury lists may be used to reconstruct historical facts, not as
   contemporaneously observed 2021 designations. No 2023+ unemployment,
   realized credit claims, awards or post-treatment projects enter controls.

Compare the old nine-control model with one expanded, prespecified model.
Report full-sample overlap balance and ESS, heldout subgroup balance/support,
training-state CV and heldout outcome prediction, pre-period placebo, and
heldout continuous forest-ranking QINI with state-level inference.
Also evaluate the frozen old ranking with expanded adjustment to distinguish
adjustment from relearning. All three outcomes are retained. Holm-adjust all
six primary ranking tests (two rankings x three outcomes); no search for a
significant outcome, state, bandwidth, covariate subset or forest seed.

Forests use the existing GRF settings and fixed train/test states. All old and
new features can modify effects in the expanded forest. Outcome regressions
are fitted only on training states with arm-specific ridge and training-state
CV. Propensity uses state plus controls without outcomes. Exact unpenalized
overlap weighting is attempted; separation/concentration is reported, not
concealed by dropping observations. Report any necessary regularization
separately. Outcome-free numerical rule: if either the full or training
unpenalized fit has any propensity outside [.01,.99] or a per-observation
gradient above 1e-6, use a fixed .01 mean-log-likelihood ridge penalty on
covariates (not state intercepts) in BOTH fits and in bootstrap refits.
This trades exact mean balance for stability and is a changed overlap target.
The same rule is evaluated in the baseline comparison; legacy estimates remain.
Inference holds trained forest and outcome nuisance fixed and
refits propensity in evaluation-state bootstrap draws. Only ten evaluation
states remain: additional covariates do not create independent variation.
Conditional parallel trends, no differential concurrent shocks and overlap
remain identifying assumptions. Better balance is not proof of causality.
